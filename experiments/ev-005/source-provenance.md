## Reading

experiment (main series: Sonnet 5; crossover: Opus 5). Verbatim memorization of these specific
trees is therefore not a plausible mechanism, which addresses the pre-exposure defect class the

planted in the model.

## Registered caveats (limitations, not controls)

1. **Idiom familiarity is not excluded.** These repositories belong to one family ecosystem and
   share conventions, layout, and prose style with each other and with material that may predate
   the cutoff. Familiarity of *style* lifts all three arms toward the ceiling; it does not
   favour one arm.
2. **`created_at` is the repository's creation date.** It does not prove the content had no
   earlier private existence elsewhere. No stronger claim is made.
3. The operative response to ceiling effects from any source remains the §7 pilot difficulty
   band (B+ task-level proportion ≥ 0.9 or ≤ 0.05 halts the main series pending owner review),
   not this table.
